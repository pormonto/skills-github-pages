A contemplation about the future:
I've been thinking about how I want to take things going into the future. When looking things in the retrospect, I feel a sense of disappointment, but when I look into the future, the spread is both hope and despair. The crowd wiles in its narcissism. The worst thing to happen was for me to lead myself into the future. No. It has been to let others to decide my future for me. My myriad of paths only exists due to the influence of others. However, I cannot ignore my weaknesses. Self-indulgence was created a situation where none of the paths were explored to any depth. Yes. That is the issue. All these extra 'paths' that were not considered 'paths' were a weakness created by the influence of others as well. The influence of others was more than just the directions for time spent in the 'category' of career paths. Yes, rather, their influence created an opening for time to spent in other directions under the guise of it not being a path at all. It being a different 'category'. These categories are completely meaningless to the flow of time. Therefore, it is best to rid of myself of the concept of categories altogether as to leave no openings to the demons that linger. Yes. This is the direction that must be taken. All is the same in the mesh of time. Categories of time are no more. Only prominence of value, void of any categorization. All assumptions of value must be tested under this. A new form of categorization my emerge despite the attempt to remove it. Yet, what will arise will be better yielded for the power of my locality.
